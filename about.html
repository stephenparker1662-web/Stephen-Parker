About

Stephen Parker

The best way to describe me is "Welly deep". I've lived and worked in the city for much of my life — old enough to remember the Wahine storm, and young enough to ride my Santa Cruz Megatower mountain bike through the fantastic trail network that weaves through the city's natural amphitheatre.

I'm also of the Beltway. I've worked in radio and television news in and out of Parliament, and held government roles in and out of the Beehive.

Wellington can be a windy and rugged city. It is also surrounded by bush and birdlife. There is always something to witness, document, and to tell.
