About

Stephen Parker

I'm a photographer and journalist based in Wellington, New Zealand — a city I've come to love for its wind, its harbour, its fierce creative energy, and its refusal to take itself too seriously.

My photography spans nature, travel, and photojournalism. Whether I'm in the Remutakas chasing the last light, on the road documenting life in motion, or in Washington tracking the mechanics of political power, I'm trying to do the same thing: bear witness and tell the truth of what I find.

On the writing side, I cover beltway politics — Congress, the White House, and the sprawling machinery of American governance. I'm interested in the gap between what Washington says and what it does, and in the human stories that get swallowed by the noise of the news cycle.

Wellington life runs through everything I do. The city is small enough to know but large enough to surprise you. I document it as honestly as I can — its people, its streets, its coast, its culture.
