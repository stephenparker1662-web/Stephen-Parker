About Stephen

The best way to describe me is "Welly deep" — old enough to remember the Wahine storm, young enough to ride a Santa Cruz Megatower through the city's natural amphitheatre. I've worked in radio and television news in and out of Parliament, and held government roles in and out of the Beehive. Wellington can be a windy and rugged city. There is always something to witness, document, and to tell.
